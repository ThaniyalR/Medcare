PRIVACY & SAFETY

Care information deserves care.

This Nutrix starter demonstrates patient and care-team registration, telephone verification, professional review and payment workflows. It is not a certified medical-record system or a substitute for professional medical advice.

Before entering real health information

Deploy only over HTTPS. Configure SMS and payment credentials as server-side secrets, use a protected production database with encryption at rest and backups, apply role-based access controls, retention and audit policies, and complete a privacy and security review for the laws in your jurisdiction. The included local SQLite development database is not encrypted at rest and should contain demo data only.

Professional accounts

SMS verification proves control of a phone number, not a professional qualification. Nurses and caretakers remain pending until an administrator independently checks their registration or certificate. Do not approve a professional based only on the value they enter.

Payments

Nutrix creates Razorpay orders on the server and verifies payment signatures before marking a care booking as paid. Configure the production webhook and confirm refunds, taxes, consumer disclosures and payout arrangements separately before taking real payments.
